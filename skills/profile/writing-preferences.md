### Voice and Tone

- **Voice:** (Active / Passive / Mixed)
  - Active: "We performed the analysis..."
  - Passive: "The analysis was performed..."
  - Mixed: Active in Introduction/Discussion, Passive in Methods/Results
- **Tone:** (Formal / Technical / Accessible / Conversational)
  - Formal: Standard for medical and natural science journals
  - Technical: Dense terminology, assumes expert audience
  - Accessible: Explains jargon, suitable for interdisciplinary audiences
- **Person:** (First person plural "we" / Third person / Impersonal)
- **Hedging Level:** (Conservative / Moderate / Direct)
  - Conservative: "This may suggest..." / "It is possible that..."
  - Moderate: "These findings suggest..." / "This indicates..."
  - Direct: "This demonstrates..." / "This confirms..."
